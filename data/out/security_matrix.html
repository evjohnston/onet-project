National security matrix

Every STEM occupation placed on three axes at once: what AI deployment buys, what it costs to take the human out of the loop, and how hard the human capability would be to rebuild afterwards. The first two say whether a handoff is attractive. The third says whether it is reversible — and that is the question a planner actually has to answer.

A 3D scatter is a poor instrument for reading exact values: near marks hide far ones and depth is ambiguous. That is why the three face views exist — they drop perspective and give an axis-aligned 2D scatter, which is where values should be read. The 3D view is for the shape of the cloud. Colour encodes severity on a validated single-hue ordinal ramp, not field; position already carries all three axes, and seven fields cannot be given seven distinguishable hues.
